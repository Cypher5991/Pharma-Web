Our Products

Discover Our Comprehensive Product Range

At Allrite Group, we deliver a wide range of oral and external pharmaceutical formulations, produced under stringent GMP and GLP-certified processes. From tablets and capsules to liquids, sachets, soaps, external and cosmetic lotions — we meet diverse healthcare demands with scale and quality.

Tablets

Manufacturing capacity over 1.2 billion tablets monthly, adhering to strict GMP and GLP standards to ensure precision, safety, and quality.

Capsules

Manufacturing capacity over 200 million capsules each month with advanced equipment and controlled processes for consistent dosing and reliability.

Oral Liquids

Manufacturing capacity over 15 million oral liquid per month supported by a robust purified water system.

Ointments

Our cutting-edge Unit-I Block A facility includes 8 meticulously designed manufacturing areas.

Sachets

Manufacturing capacity over 7.5 million sachets monthly, providing convenient single-dose packaging for powders and granules.

Soaps

Manufacturing capacity over 18 million medicated, cosmetic and ayush soaps at facility crafted to meet the highest manufacturing standards.

Cosmetic

Dermatological lotions produced with GMP and GLP standards.
